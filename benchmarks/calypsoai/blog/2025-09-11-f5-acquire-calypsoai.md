## F5 to Acquire CalypsoAI to Bring Advanced AI Guardrails to Large Enterprises

F5 announced its intention to acquire CalypsoAI, an enterprise AI security pioneer. The acquisition aims to integrate CalypsoAI's platform capabilities into F5's Application Delivery and Security Platform (ADSP) to create a comprehensive solution for securing AI inference.

### CEO Quote

Francois Locoh-Donou, President and CEO of F5, stated: "AI is redefining enterprise architecture -- and the attack surface companies must defend." He emphasized that traditional firewalls cannot adequately address emerging threats.

### Business Rationale

Enterprises are embedding AI across products and operations, creating an expanded attack surface spanning applications, agents, APIs, models, and data stores. Security teams face sophisticated new threats while managing regulatory compliance requirements and uncontrolled shadow AI usage.

### Key Capabilities

CalypsoAI's platform delivers three core functions:

1. **Adversarial threat protection** -- Defense against prompt injection and jailbreak attacks with real-time threat management and proactive red-teaming testing against over 10,000 new attack prompts monthly

2. **Secure data** -- Runtime detection and prevention of sensitive data leakage and policy violations with AI interaction auditing

3. **Unified visibility and governance** -- Centralized observability, policy control, and audit logging supporting GDPR and EU AI Act compliance

### CalypsoAI CEO Commentary

Donnchadh Casey, CEO of CalypsoAI, highlighted: "Enterprises want to move fast with AI while reducing the risk of data leaks, unsafe outputs, or compliance failures."
