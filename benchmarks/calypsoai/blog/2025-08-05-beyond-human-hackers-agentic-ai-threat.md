## Beyond Human Hackers: Agentic AI Becomes the Primary Threat Actor

CalypsoAI announced that autonomous AI agents have become the primary threat actors in cybersecurity, surpassing human hackers in capability. The company revealed this finding through its August CalypsoAI Security Index (CASI) Leaderboard, which demonstrated measurable impact.

### Main Finding

"Every leading AI model suffered a 12.5% drop in its security score," marking the first time agentic systems demonstrated the ability to compromise major AI models at scale.

### Technical Capabilities

The threat research uses **Signature Attack Packs**, a feature within CalypsoAI's Inference Red-Team product. According to the announcement, "Red-Team's fully autonomous agent researches, generates, tests, and curates a new set of high-severity adversarial prompts" monthly.

### Product Enhancements

**Red-Team Updates:**
- **Agentic Fingerprints**: 공격 에이전트의 행동, 의사결정, 추론 경로에 대한 가시성 제공
- **Red-Team On-Premises**: 에어갭 환경을 위한 온프레미스 버전 얼리 액세스 제공

**Defend Updates:**
- **Outcome Analysis**: 프롬프트와 응답이 플래그/차단된 이유를 명확히 하는 기능

### Leadership Perspective

CTO James White stated: "We now have the capability to conduct threat research, build intel and generate new and novel attacks...all with agents doing the work."

CEO Donnchadh Casey emphasized: "Understanding where AI systems encounter vulnerabilities, make bad decisions, and why and how they fail is absolutely critical."

- 자율 AI 에이전트가 사이버보안에서 인간 해커를 능가하는 주요 위협 행위자로 부상

- 에이전틱 시스템이 대규모로 AI 모델을 침해할 수 있음을 최초 입증
