## August 2025 Release: Expanding the Boundaries of AI Security with Major Enhancements

CalypsoAI's August release introduces significant capabilities across its AI Security Platform, focusing on observability, compliance, real-time defense, and red-teaming functionalities.

---

## Key Features and Enhancements

### Splunk Integration

The platform now offers native Splunk integration enabling enterprises to:

- Consolidate AI threat events alongside existing network, endpoint, and cloud telemetry
- Trigger automated alerts and SOAR workflows when high-risk AI activity occurs
- Streamline compliance through seamless log retention and audit processes

### Inference Defend

**Outcome Analysis:** This capability provides transparency into threat detection by revealing which scanner triggered alerts and what specific content or rule violations occurred. Security teams gain access to timestamps and user activity traces for improved incident response.

**EU AI Act Scanner Package:** A new compliance-focused tool addresses Article 5's prohibited risk categories:
- Manipulation
- Social Scoring
- Biometric Data Harvesting
- Surveillance

The package offers "immediate, out-of-the-box baseline coverage" for organizations building GenAI applications in or serving the EU market.

**Custom Scanner Versioning:** Teams can now create multiple scanner versions, test them in parallel, and manage full lifecycle controls including publication, rollback, and project-level enforcement.

### Inference Red-Team

**Agentic Fingerprints:** An interactive visualization system displays multi-turn AI attack progression, including each decision, prompt, response, and the reasoning behind each step.

**Agentic Signature Attack Packs:** Monthly packages generated by autonomous agents research, create, test, and deliver new high-impact attacks.

**Enhanced Error Messaging:** Improved notifications for API key expiration, rate limits, and connection issues.

**Red-Team On-Prem (Early Access):** Organizations with strict data residency requirements will gain access to on-premise deployment options.

---

## Impact Statement

The release demonstrates CalypsoAI's commitment to "proactive, enterprise-ready AI security" by unifying detection, prevention, red-teaming, and compliance functions at the inference layer.

- Splunk 네이티브 통합: AI 위협 이벤트를 기존 보안 텔레메트리와 통합
